> **{{ page.title }}**
>
> Verify may loop indefinitely in the latest RSI Launcher on Linux. This is a known issue and CIG is working on it.
>
> Press the install button instead of verifying.
>
> If you need to verify files, you can safely cancel it after it completes once and proceed to launch the game. The first loop will have completely successfully.
>
> The launcher may download a file of about 2.5 GB - 5 GB during every verify. this is normal.
